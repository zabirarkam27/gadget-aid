# Repair tools, gallery and discount-points policies

## Repair decisions and discovery

The repair/replace calculator runs locally, accepts same-currency user costs and assumed usable months, and shows the arithmetic. It does not predict lifetime, safety, resale value, environmental savings or financial certainty. Amounts allow at most two decimals; months are integers 1–120. The listed-price estimator uses bounded matching real visible service records, not external AI or final bills. Providers may publish up to 20 optional model/repair prices per service in USD. A requested model must match a published model after Unicode normalization, case folding and whitespace removal; punctuation and Pro/Plus variants remain distinct. Every repair keyword must match the same rate's repair label. Model and repair filtering happens before sampling up to 100 visible services. Unsupported combinations return no price, without substituting a general listing price. Leaving the model blank uses general catalogue prices, whose existing `$` display does not establish currency. All results are non-binding; provider rates do not change booking prices or the final USD quotation. No currency conversion, invented market data or production price backfill is introduced.

Comparison accepts at most three canonical service IDs and returns the same allowlisted fields for each. Hidden/removed/restricted-provider listings are omitted. No invented rating, availability or warranty is used. Bookmarks are private UID-scoped records, one per technician per owner, max 100, paginated. They anchor to a real visible service; an unavailable anchor yields a generic unavailable bookmark until the owner removes/re-saves it. No public bookmark counts or identity sharing.

Recycling is informational only. No GadgetAid collection partner, pickup or device disposal service exists. Manufacturer/local-authority verification is required before handing over a device. General external references are [US EPA electronics guidance](https://www.epa.gov/recycle/electronics-donation-and-recycling) and [CPSO battery safety](https://www.consumerproductsafety.gov.sg/consumers/product-safety-tips/products-that-charge-or-power-appliances/safety-tips-on-lithium-ion-batteries/). They are not Bangladesh-specific collection rules or partnerships. The app does not tell users to open electronics or remove built-in/damaged batteries.

## Before/after publication

Only the canonical approved provider of a trusted completed handover for another customer can propose one example per booking. Legacy status text alone is insufficient; modern UID ownership, BSON dates and trusted workflow events are required. Provider title/description are allowlisted/bounded; known customer names, contact/record IDs and email/phone-shaped text are rejected. This is an additional filter, not reliable image/text anonymization.

Lifecycle: private draft → both images uploaded → provider submits an immutable proposal → exact customer consents → pending moderation → recently signed-in Admin/Super Admin inspects that revision with reason/confirmation → audited publication. No edits or image replacement are permitted after submission. Corrections require deleting the proposal and starting again with new customer consent. The customer can decline; either participant can withdraw/delete. Restricted/former providers can still withdraw their own example. Approval is never a certification of repair quality.

Provider confirms rights to supply each image for private review. JPEG/PNG only, 3 MiB raw each, strict magic/decode/dimension limits and re-encoding via the existing safe image pipeline. Original bytes/names/metadata are not stored; visible faces, names, screens, labels/serials must be removed before upload and checked by both customer and moderator. At-rest AES-GCM uses the existing server key with a separate `gallery:` authenticated context; it never reuses receipt URLs or receipt consent. Losing the key prevents image recovery.

Draft/review media is readable only by the two booking participants or a verified Admin after a recent, audited exact-revision inspection. Publication exposes only opaque example ID, approved title/description, public provider name and publication time; public image URLs contain no booking, customer or device IDs. Each public GET rechecks consent, state and current listing/provider visibility. It uses no-store/nosniff and a restrictive image CSP. Approved public photos may still be copied by viewers; withdrawal cannot erase outside copies or retained infrastructure backups. Customers see this before granting public-display consent.

Retention: examples and encrypted images stay until a participant deletes/withdraws, or private declined media is erased immediately. Confirmed withdrawal deletes proposal and both images from the primary database atomically. Minimal separate admin audit records remain, without image bytes, captions, customer or device identifiers. Infrastructure backup/key retention follows existing operator policy; no backup erasure guarantee. Ten examples/provider, 20 MiB saved images/provider, 100 MiB total platform image capacity, 10 successful image uploads/minute/provider, two in-flight normalizations per server instance. Shared transactional counters prevent successful writes bypassing byte/rate quotas; this is not deployment-wide traffic protection.

## Discount points — discount-points-v1

- Earning: verified, unrestricted customer; first eligible completed repair per Bangladesh day earns 20 points. Eligible means a new server-marked trusted handover by a different approved provider, customer-approved quote total at least USD 20 and labor at least USD 10. Existing historical or unquoted legacy completions are not backfilled. Projection runs idempotently from at most 25 unchecked handovers per wallet read and records whether more remain. The authoritative completion timestamp, not wallet-read time, is the issue date.
- Maturity/expiry: available after 7 × 24 hours, expires after 365 × 24 hours from that handover. All balances are promotional, non-cash, nontransferable. Expired credits remain in private history for accounting, but cannot be spent.
- Referral: one immutable inviter choice before any booking; both sides must have verified accounts. Cryptographically random referral codes reveal no UID/email. No self-referral/cycles; chain checks are bounded. The first qualifying completion with a provider other than the inviter can issue 40 points to the inviter; at most three referral credits/referrer/Bangladesh calendar month. Restricted inviters are excluded. Merely signing up/claiming a code earns nothing.
- Redemption: 100 available unexpired points = 5% off labor only, rounded down to cents, capped at USD 5. No discount on parts. A provider explicitly opts into funding each quotation; no provider enrolment or platform reimbursement is implied. The customer must explicitly authorize point redemption as well as quotation approval. Old API clients cannot silently spend points by approving without that flag.
- Accounting: wallet guards serialize credits/debits. Debits and repair approval commit in the same transaction; failed or insufficient-balance approval changes neither. Spend closest-to-expiry credits first, one redemption per booking. Pending/rejected/superseded quotes do not reserve points; the balance is rechecked on approval. Each immutable offer preserves original labor, discount, policy, funding actor/time and resulting quote total.
- Cancellation: unexpired allocated points restore once, atomically with a supported cancellation; their original expiry is unchanged. Expired allocations are not renewed. Ledger records points restored and prevents duplicate restoration. No cash refund or payment transaction occurs. Completed bookings cannot be cancelled by this workflow.
- Abuse/operations: canonical ownership/handover and customer approval, verification, source IDs, self-provider exclusion, maturity and daily/monthly caps reduce abuse. They do not establish payment proof or eliminate multi-account/colluding-provider abuse. Discounts are limited and voluntarily provider-funded. Existing audited account restrictions stop new earning/claims/redemption; private balances/history remain readable. No arbitrary cash adjustment, credit transfer or automatic payment API is added.

Collections: `savedTechnicians`, `repairExamples`, `repairExampleFiles`, `loyaltyProfiles`, `loyaltyCredits`, `loyaltySpends`; existing `operationMeta` guards/counters and source bookings are updated transactionally. Ledger history retains minimal owner/source references for accounting; user DTOs omit source customer/booking identifiers on referral credits. No prompt, passport, receipt, serial or email is copied into these public outputs. No external message is sent automatically. Production backup/retention and abuse review remain operational responsibilities.

Before a large-scale rollout review query plans and provision indexes on savedTechnicians(ownerUid,_id), examples(state,customerConsent,_id), exampleFiles(exampleId)/ownerUid, loyaltyProfiles(code unique), loyaltyCredits(ownerUid,expiresAt,availableAt)/ownerUid,type,day/month and loyaltySpends(ownerUid,_id). The deployment must support MongoDB transactions and the existing image key/runtime. Do not seed synthetic rewards or images into production; keep test identities and data isolated.
